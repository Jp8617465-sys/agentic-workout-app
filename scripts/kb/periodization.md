# Periodization Structure

## Current Mesocycle
- **Model:** Block periodization
- **Goal:** Concurrent strength + fat loss
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Structure:** 3-week accumulation → 3-week intensification → 2-week realization → 1-week deload (repeat)

## Phase Targets

| Phase | Sets | Reps | RPE | Rest |
|-------|------|------|-----|------|
| Accumulation | 3–4 | 8–10 | 6.5–7.5 | 2–3 min |
| Intensification | 4–5 | 4–6 | 7.5–8.5 | 3–4 min |
| Realization | 3–4 | 1–3 | 8.5–9.5 | 4–5 min |
| Deload | 2–3 | 8–10 | 5–6 | 2 min |

## Session Split (3–4×/week)
- **Day A:** Lower — Squat pattern, Hip hinge, Accessory
- **Day B:** Upper — Horizontal push, Horizontal pull, Vertical pull
- **Day C (optional):** Full body — 1 lower compound + upper compounds at reduced volume

## Key Exercises (constraint-aware)
- **Primary:** Romanian deadlift, incline bench press, cable row, lat pulldown
- **Avoid:** Conventional deadlift (low back), overhead press (shoulder - monitor), jump variations (right ankle/plantar)
- **Rotate in when resolved:** Bulgarian split squat, single-leg calf work
